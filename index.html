Core Features

Built for the Underworld

Every mechanic, every mission, every bullet designed to deliver a cinematic crime empire experience with no compromises.

Gun War Combat

Real-time ballistic combat with 8 weapon classes — pistols, SMGs, shotguns, sniper rifles, explosives, and more. Precision hit detection with cinematic kill cams.

Bank Heist Missions

Orchestrate vault breaches, crowd control, and getaway sequences across five fully designed bank environments. Timed explosives, guard AI, and loot splits.

Real-Time Multiplayer

Up to 100 players per session across WebSocket-powered rooms. Gang vs gang, free-for-all, and co-op heist modes with live leaderboards.

12 Distinct Locations

Choose from 4 rival factions — each with unique perks, weapons, and storylines. Territory control shifts dynamically based on match outcomes.

How It Plays

Every Match, a War Story

  • 01

    Choose Your Faction

    Pick your gang, load your loadout, and enter the city. Loyalty determines your allies — and your enemies.

  • 02

    Seize the Territory

    Control zones grant income and resources. Hold them long enough and your faction rules the city.

  • 03

    Execute the Heist

    The bank mission requires team coordination — breach, secure, extract. Split the loot or take it all.

  • 04

    Rise to Godfather

    XP, kills, and successful heists push your rank. The streets remember who earns their title.
